## Why

Nothing to plan from until these were settled. A static deployment can't be produced inside a Worker, and the repo has no remote, so "regenerate every cron" had no mechanism behind it. Owner chose Cloudflare Workers Builds, triggered by the ingest cron through a Deploy Hook, building `main` from a public GitHub repo named `915tldr-frontend`.

## Issues Encountered

- The v2 tracer re-derives slugs with a function that doesn't match v1's (`don't` gives `don-t` instead of `dont`, and there's no 100-character cap). Built from the full corpus, those URLs would miss nine months of indexed links. Recorded as locked decision D-07.
- `/changelog`'s history comes from `915tldr.com2/public/changelog.json` (12 entries), not D1. The D1 table `public_changelogs` holds 6 orphaned rows from December 2025. Both are kept (D-13).
