Facts from planning:
- The rule to remove is `header:not([data-block]) { border-bottom: var(--rule-thick) solid var(--ink); }`, which is black in light and near-white in dark.
- The spectrum (`[data-spectrum]`) is a body-level sibling immediately after the header on all five pages, and stays full-bleed.
- The toggle is a body-level `<button data-theme-toggle>` between nav and main on every page, with `margin: var(--space-3) var(--space-4)`. At ≥80em, header, nav, main and footer get `max-width: 80rem; margin: 0 auto`, but the toggle does not. That is defect 10.
- The fix is CSS-only, so no markup or tab order changes. A percentage margin resolves against body's content width, which excludes the scrollbar: `margin-inline-start: calc(max(0px, (100% - 80rem) / 2) + var(--space-4))` aligns the toggle with the header content edge.
- External links at planning time (re-count with grep first): article.html has 2 (AI disclosure and original-reporting links to ktsm.com, `rel="noopener external"`), and contact.html has 2 (jjaimealeman.com and 915website.com, `rel="noopener"`). There are no external links in the footer.
- Playwright: `page.route` does not intercept a popup's first request. Route at the browser-context level instead, and fulfil with a stub so nothing leaves the machine.
- The spanish-overflow layout loop cannot inject into a visually hidden element, because its `visible` check would fail by design. The cue is marked `assistiveOnly` and gets its own accessible-name test.
</context>
