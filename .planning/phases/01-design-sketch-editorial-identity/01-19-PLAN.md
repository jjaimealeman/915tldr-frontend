<objective>
Close revision request 3 (article whitespace at desktop) and the article part of request 7 (full width at 768px).

Purpose: at 1280px the article was a single 68ch column beside a large empty space. The owner asked how other news sites handle this; the direction agreed in session was a right rail at ≥1024px with "Latest" and "More in {category}", no ads, and the body measure kept near 70ch. At 768px the owner wants the text to span the full width. This plan introduces the reusable layout pieces that 01-20 applies to the changelog: `[data-layout="with-rail"]`, `[data-reading-column]` and `[data-rail]`.

Output: rail markup, layout CSS with a new 64em breakpoint, a geometry spec, content-integrity checks, and a Spanish rail heading.
</objective>
