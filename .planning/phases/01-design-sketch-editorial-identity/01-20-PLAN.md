Close revision request 4 (changelog layout bug and whitespace), request 5 (contact centring and the button/heading spacing bug), and the changelog and contact parts of request 7 (full width at 768px).

Planning traced this to the ≥80em rule `[data-dispatch] { display: grid; grid-template-columns: 12rem 1fr }`, which auto-places the date, title and body into two columns and so drops the body under the date. The owner labelled the screenshot 768px, while the rule applies from 80em (1280px at 16px), so the executor measures the real widths first and reports what it finds. Contact needs a centred column and real space before "Latest Stories".

Output: explicit dispatch placement, the changelog on the with-rail layout, a centred rem-width contact column, a spacing fix, and geometry specs for both pages.
</objective>
