<objective>
Replace the tracer's initial hue values with the real D-02 palette. Each hue is sampled from a real photograph of the place, and all eight hues sit on shared OKLCH lightness stops, with a third dark-theme ramp (D-03). Checked against the final D-04 neutrals, the palette passes the contrast gate with margin.

Purpose: the category palette is the most visible design decision in the phase and the one most constrained by accessibility — PROJECT.md says the palette is constrained by contrast, not the reverse. Owner constraint C-01 narrows the register to the saturated end of El Paso and Juárez.

Output: photo provenance, sampled hues, a re-runnable palette generator, the regenerated palette sub-regions in style.css, a palette report, and swatch evidence for the owner.
</objective>

**Gamut note — planner's resolution of how D-02 meets physics (reversible):** at a fixed lightness, the largest chroma sRGB can show depends on hue. Cyan and yellow-green run out first, blue and magenta last. "Fixed lightness/chroma stops" is therefore implemented as fixed lightness plus a shared requested chroma. Each hue's chroma is lowered with culori `clampChroma(color, 'oklch')` only as far as the gamut forces, which keeps L and hue. Every written value is inside sRGB, so it renders identically on sRGB and P3 screens, and the contrast gate checks exactly what renders. Any reduction to below 60% of the requested chroma is flagged in the report, because that hue will look duller than its siblings.

**Known C-01 risk to surface, not to hide:** at the text-safe block lightness, the orange and yellow family (hue about 40–100°) turns dark amber or olive, which can read as brown. The report flags every block stop in that band for the owner's judgement at approval. Do not "fix" it by quietly leaving those categories out of the block tier; D-01 applies to all eight.

**Proposed category assignment** (carried from 01-02; re-assign only if a photograph forces it, and record why): crime = ocotillo red; politics = indigo night sky; sports = storefront or neon orange (UTEP orange association); business = marigold; education = violet sunset; community = Juárez storefront magenta or pink; health = sotol or creosote green; weather = hard turquoise daylight sky.
