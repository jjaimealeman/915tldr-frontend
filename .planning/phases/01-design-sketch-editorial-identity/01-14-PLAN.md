<objective>
Close owner decision D-GAP-B (Source Serif 4 bold headlines; Instrument Serif only for the wordmark) and part 2 of D-GAP-A (shrink the Source Serif 4 subsets).

Purpose: the owner's screenshot showed a headline computed as "Instrument Serif – 700". Instrument Serif ships one weight, so that bold was synthesised by the browser. The two decisions interact. Once headlines leave Instrument Serif, its italic face serves nothing and can be retired. And pinning Source Serif 4's opsz axis is the only lever that meaningfully shrinks the subsets. Dropping hinting changed nothing (+28 bytes; the source is unhinted). Restricting opsz to a 12–60 range saved only 1.7%. A smaller preloaded file is also more likely to arrive inside the short `optional` block period.

Output: headline tokens and rules, a structure spec that proves the new type roles, a tested fvar reader, a smaller font build with a lever report, and the dated document amendments.

Spanish width calibration is stale after this plan, because the fonts' widths change. 01-15 recalibrates it. Do not run spanish-overflow.spec.ts here.
