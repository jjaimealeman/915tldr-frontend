<objective>
Close owner decision D-GAP-A, part 1 of 2: switch to `font-display: optional` (keeping the preloads) and re-measure criterion 5 honestly. Part 2, shrinking the subsets, is 01-14.

Purpose: 01-09 showed that swap-triggered reflow is inherent under `swap`. The owner chose option C. Under `optional`, a browser either renders the webfont from first paint or keeps the fallback for that page view, so a mid-render swap should never happen. "The shift is now zero" is only evidence if we can show which path each load took, and that the instrument still detects a real swap when one happens. Without both, a zero could just mean the instrument went blind.

`optional` also creates a new silent-failure risk for this harness. A webfont that misses the short block period is never used for that document, so any width measurement can silently measure the fallback. This plan guards against that.

Output: the regenerated fonts region, a classified measurement with a positive control, a strict evidence gate, a harness guard, the PRD §6.5 amendment, and updated ledger entries.
</objective>
