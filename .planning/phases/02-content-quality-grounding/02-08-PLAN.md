<objective>
Make the grounding check's accuracy a measured pair of numbers rather than an anecdote, then
wire it into live ingest so a flagged summary is retried once and, failing that, held with a
visible count.

Purpose: a detector validated against a single known positive and zero negatives says nothing
about its false-positive rate, and D-09 makes false positives expensive — a false positive
silently kills a real article. D-10 asks for the same instinct Phase 1 applied to its stress set
and its generated contrast table: make the acceptance bar executable and re-runnable, so a
prompt or model change re-proves it instead of eroding it.

Output: a labelled fixture set drawn from the real corpus, a re-runnable calibration test with a
recorded ceiling, live-ingest gating with one stricter retry, and a review queue.
