Teach the build/sync half of the archive tier about Spanish pages: tier facts per language,
partition entries on the language-qualified R2 keys from 06-02, R2 key validation and listing

uuid-only collision research found.

blow-up) or be uploaded under a key the Worker never reads. Output: changes to `tier-facts.ts`,
`partition-archive.mjs`, `r2-client.ts`, `archive-sync.mjs`, `kv-manifest.ts`, and tests.
</objective>
