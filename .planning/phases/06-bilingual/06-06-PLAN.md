<objective>
Bring Spanish content into the build: a second Content Layer collection (`articlesEs`) loaded at
build time from `article_translations`, joined to the English collection by uuid in a pure helper
the templates use. The English collection and every page that reads it stay untouched.

Purpose: the render side of I18N-01 and the data every `/es` page (I18N-04) needs, under the
Core Value — D1 is read only at build time through the single permitted module. Output: two D1
query functions, the Spanish loader, collection registration, a Spanish never-shrink baseline,
and `spanish-view.ts`.
</objective>
