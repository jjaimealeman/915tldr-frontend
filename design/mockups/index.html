New Study Confirms T. rex Was Warm-Blooded, Changing Our Understanding of Dinosaurs

Recent research has confirmed that Tyrannosaurus rex had a body temperature of approximately 97 degrees Fahrenheit, indicating it was warm-blooded. This finding, published in the journal Science Advances, is based on chemical analysis of fossilized teeth and adds to evidence that T. rex could thrive in colder climates. The study enhances our understanding of the dinosaur's behavior, physiology, and ecological interactions during the Late Cretaceous period, around 69 to 66 million years ago. Researchers believe this new method could be applied to other dinosaur species, potentially reshaping our views on their metabolic rates and lifestyles.

Key Details:

  • T. rex body temperature estimated at 97°F (36°C), similar to humans.
  • Study published in Science Advances provides direct evidence of warm-bloodedness.
  • Research utilized fossilized teeth from a T. rex skeleton at the Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County.
  • Technique could be applied to other dinosaurs to explore their metabolic rates.

EPISD Raises Tax Rate, Impacting Homeowners in El Paso

The El Paso Independent School District (EPISD) board has approved a new tax rate that will increase the average homeowner's tax bill by approximately $114 annually. The new rate is set at $1.0745 per $100 of taxable property valuation, which is slightly below the maximum rate allowed by voters. This change raises the average tax due on homes from $1,179 last year to $1,294.18 this year, reflecting a rise in average taxable home values from $109,167 to $120,455. Homeowners should be aware of exemptions available, including a $140,000 exemption for most homeowners and additional exemptions for seniors and disabled individuals.

Key Details:

  • New tax rate approved at $1.0745 per $100 of property valuation.
  • Average homeowner's tax bill increases from $1,179 to $1,294.18.
  • First $140,000 of property value is exempt from school taxes for most homeowners.
  • Additional $60,000 exemption for seniors and disabled individuals.
  • Veterans with 100% disability rating are exempt from all property taxes.

Crisis de agua en EE.UU.: Niveles históricos bajos en embalses clave afectan a millones

Los niveles de agua en Lake Powell y Lake Mead han alcanzado cifras históricamente bajas, lo que representa un grave problema para el suministro de agua y energía en el suroeste de EE.UU. A partir del 12 de julio, Lake Powell estaba a 3,524 pies y Lake Mead a 1,043 pies, rompiendo récords anteriores. Estos embalses son cruciales, ya que abastecen agua a 40 millones de personas y generan electricidad para 700,000 hogares. Con el 95% de la cuenca del río Colorado en sequía, se prevé que los niveles sigan cayendo, lo que podría llevar a una crisis en la gestión del agua entre los siete estados que dependen de este recurso. Las negociaciones para compartir el agua se complican, y se espera que nuevas directrices operativas se publiquen en las próximas semanas para abordar la crisis.

Key Details:

  • Lake Powell actualmente al 23% de su capacidad, y Lake Mead al 27%.
  • Se espera que ambos embalses establezcan nuevos mínimos históricos diariamente hasta la primavera de 2024.
  • Se impuso una fecha límite en febrero para que los estados llegaran a un acuerdo sobre la gestión del agua, pero no se alcanzó un consenso.
  • El Secretario del Interior, Doug Burgum, se reunió con los gobernadores de los siete estados para discutir soluciones.
  • Se anticipa que El Niño podría traer alivio a la sequía en otoño e invierno.
