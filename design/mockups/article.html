USPS Launches Operation Santa, Inviting El Pasoans to Adopt Holiday Letters

The U.S. Postal Service has begun its annual Operation Santa, inviting residents to send letters to Santa Claus.

This cherished initiative, which has been running since 1912, allows individuals to adopt letters from children and fulfill their holiday wishes. This year, El Pasoans are encouraged to participate and spread joy during the festive season. The program not only brings smiles to children's faces but also fosters community spirit by connecting donors with those in need.
