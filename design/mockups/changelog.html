Fredd Young, NMSU Football Legend and NFL Star, Passes Away at 64

Fredd Young, a celebrated Hall of Famer from New Mexico State University (NMSU) and former Seattle Seahawks player, has died at the age of 64. Young was a standout linebacker for the Aggies from 1980 to 1983, setting a record with six sacks in a single game against LA Tech in 1983. Drafted by the Seahawks in 1984, he played seven seasons in the NFL, participating in 101 games and starting 79. His contributions to football and the Las Cruces community were recognized in 1991 when he was inducted into the U.S. Bank/NM State Athletics Hall of Fame. His legacy is remembered fondly by NMSU Athletics, highlighting his significant impact on the sport and local community.

KVIA · Sept. 16, 12:39 p.m.

El Paso Animal Services Under Scrutiny Following Rescue Group's Allegations

El Paso Animal Services is currently under investigation due to serious allegations raised by the local nonprofit, Paws for Promise. The organization claims that animals returned from the city's facilities have exhibited health issues, prompting concerns about the procedures in place. A representative from Paws for Promise shared these concerns through a Facebook video, highlighting the potential impact on animal welfare. This situation is significant as it raises questions about the standards of care provided by Animal Services and the implications for local animal rescue efforts.
